# 60-second walkthrough

1. Open CardFit. The question is on the first screen, next to an example result labelled as an example.
2. Choose **Try a demo**, then **Grocery household**. The spending is fictional. The right side names a card, a yearly dollar figure, the fee, and why.
3. Open **How this was calculated**. Groceries, dining, and the other categories are listed with the rate that was actually used.
4. Switch **What do you want back?** to **Travel points** and set the fee to **No annual fee**. The list should say no travel card in the catalog has a $0 fee.
5. Choose **Balance carrier**. Interest appears because a $4,000 balance was entered, and the low-rate card can lead even though it earns no rewards.
6. Go back, choose **Upload a CSV**, and use **Download sample CSV**. Confirm that purchases are positive. The reconciliation shows payments, interest, fees, and the cash advance left out, then a monthly profile.
7. Open **Sources** for the October 1, 2026 cash-back change, the point values, and the cards that are not ranked.

Nothing in steps 2 through 6 is saved after the tab closes.
