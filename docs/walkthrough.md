# 60-second walkthrough

1. Open CardFit. The landing page states the question, and the carousel rotates through every card in the catalog with its fee and headline earn rate.
2. Scroll to **Not sure where to start?** and choose **Grocery household**. The spending is fictional. The result names one card, a yearly dollar figure after the fee, and three plain-language reasons.
3. Open **How this was calculated**. Groceries, dining, and the other categories are listed with the rate that was actually used.
4. Choose **Adjust numbers**, switch **What would you rather get back?** to **Travel rewards**, and set the fee to **No annual fee**. The page should say no travel card in the catalog has a $0 fee, with a button to widen the comparison.
5. Choose **Start over**, then **Balance carrier**. Interest appears because a $4,000 balance was entered, and the low-rate card leads as the lowest net cost even though it earns no rewards.
6. Open **What if my spending changes?** The chart and table stay hidden until asked for, and the break-even sentence names the spending level where the order changes.
7. Go back, choose **Upload a CSV**, and use **Download a sample CSV to try**. Confirm that purchases are positive. The review shows payments, interest, fees, and the cash advance left out, then a monthly profile.
8. Open **Sources and method** for the October 1, 2026 cash-back change, the point values, and the cards that are not ranked.

Nothing in steps 2 through 7 is saved after the tab closes.
